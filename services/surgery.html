Patient Profiles

Who is This Service For?

Tailored clinical protocols for different companions in Ranchi.

Young Pets (6+ Months)

Ideal age for safe preventive spaying and neutering to eliminate reproductive diseases.

Injured Animals

Trauma cases with bite wounds, vehicular cuts, or deep skin tears requiring surgical debridement.

Senior Pets

Carefully assessed pets requiring tumor removals or non-invasive soft-tissue corrective procedures.

When Should You Visit?

Planned sterilization at 6-12 months of age, visible abnormal growths or subcutaneous lumps, or sudden deep lacerations and open injuries.

Service Questions

Frequently Asked Questions: Veterinary Surgery

Direct, medically verified answers from our veterinary doctors.

Sterilization prevents uterine infections (pyometra), reduces mammary tumors in females, eliminates testicular cancer in males, and prevents aggressive territorial roaming.
Pets must typically be fasted (no food for 8-12 hours, water allowed up to 2 hours prior) to prevent vomiting during anesthesia.
Most pets recover within 7 to 10 days. An Elizabethan collar (cone) is provided to prevent the pet from licking or disturbing the incision.
All surgical procedures are conducted personally by our certified B.V.Sc veterinary surgeons (Dr. Vivek Kr. Gupta / Dr. Ankit R. Bara).
