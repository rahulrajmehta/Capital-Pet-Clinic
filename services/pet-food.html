Patient Profiles

Who is This Service For?

Tailored clinical protocols for different companions in Ranchi.

Growing Puppies & Kittens

Calorie-dense, calcium-balanced diets formulated for proper bone and muscular growth.

Pets with Medical Conditions

Clinical nutrition designed to support kidneys, dissolve urinary stones, or soothe digestive inflammation.

Adult & Senior Pets

Weight-management and joint-support formulations tailored for slower metabolisms.

When Should You Visit?

Transitioning a puppy off mother's milk, persistent soft stool from low-grade food, dull coat or skin dryness, or doctor's prescription for therapeutic diet.

Clinical Workflow

How the Procedure Works

Structured, gentle, and transparent steps from start to finish.

1

Pet Body Condition Check

Doctor checks pet weight, body condition score, and coat quality.

2

Diet Recommendation

Selection of the ideal brand, protein source, and life-stage formula.

3

Transition Protocol

A 7-day gradual mix chart (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) to prevent digestive upset.

4

Portion Measurement

Exact gram-per-day measurement based on pet activity level.
