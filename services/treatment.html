Who is This Service For?

Tailored clinical protocols for different companions in Ranchi.

Canines (Dogs)

All breeds from Indipets and Labradors to German Shepherds and Shih Tzus requiring acute medical attention.

Felines (Cats)

Gentle low-stress diagnostic care for domestic short-hairs, Persians, and kittens.

Small Pets & Birds

Specialized clinical checks for rabbits, birds, and other companion animals.

When Should You Visit?

Lethargy, refusal to eat for over 24 hours, repetitive vomiting or watery stool, high body temperature, persistent scratching or hair loss, or labored breathing.

Clinical Workflow

How the Procedure Works

Structured, gentle, and transparent steps from start to finish.

1

Symptom & History Intake

Doctor discusses pet behavior, duration of illness, diet, and previous medical history.

2

Clinical Physical Exam

Detailed evaluation of body temperature, mucus membranes, lymph nodes, lungs, and abdomen.

3

Therapy & Administration

Immediate supportive care (injections, IV drip, or wound dressing) administered in clinic.

4

Prescription & Follow-up

Clear dosage schedule dispensed with follow-up review scheduled within 48 to 72 hours.
