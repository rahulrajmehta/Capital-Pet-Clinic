Canine Medicine & Surgery

Dogs & Puppies (All Breeds)

Dogs are beloved family members. We welcome all breeds, from resilient Indian Pariah Dogs and energetic Labradors to brachycephalic Pugs and French Bulldogs requiring airway monitoring.

  • Parvo & Distemper Therapy: Emergency fluid electrolyte management with high survival outcomes.
  • Tick Fever & Blood Parasite Care: Rapid diagnostic tests for Babesia and Ehrlichia.
  • Dermatology: Treatment for fungal infections, flea allergy dermatitis, and mange.
  • Surgical Interventions: Spaying, neutering, hernia repair, and wound suturing.

Feline-Friendly Care

Cats & Kittens (Persian & Indie)

Cats are unique creatures with sensitive metabolic pathways. Our clinic provides low-stress feline handling to reduce fear and anxiety during checkups and vaccinations.

  • Tricat & Rabies Vaccination: Essential protection against Panleukopenia and Calicivirus.
  • Feline Urinary Health (FLUTD): Treatment for painful urination, blockages, and bladder crystals.
  • Cat Spaying / Castration: Minimally invasive safe sterilization for indoor health.
  • Hairball & Gastrointestinal Management: Specially formulated pastes and diets.

Avian Healthcare

Pet Birds & Parakeets

Birds instinctively hide sickness until it is advanced. Our veterinarians provide prompt avian consultations for Budgies, Cockatiels, Lovebirds, Parrots, and Pigeons in Ranchi.

  • Respiratory Infections: Tail bobbing, wheezing, and nasal discharge care.
  • Beak, Nail Trimming & Feather Care: Overgrown keratin correction and mites treatment.
  • Nutritional Balancing: Preventing seed-only vitamin A and calcium deficiencies.
  • Egg-Binding Emergencies: Life-saving stabilization for female birds.
Inquire for Bird Doctor
Small Mammal Care

Rabbits, Guinea Pigs & Hamsters

Small herbivores possess fragile digestive systems requiring high fiber and careful hydration. We assist pet parents in managing their delicate wellbeing.

  • GI Stasis Emergency: Swift intervention for rabbits that have stopped eating or pooping.
  • Incisor & Molar Malocclusion: Safe dental burring for overgrown teeth.
  • Ear Mite & Parasite Treatments: Crusting and ear inflammation relief.
  • Diet & Habitat Guidance: Proper hay-to-pellet ratio planning.

Emergency Watchlist

When to Bring Your Pet to Clinic Immediately

If you observe any of the following symptoms, do not wait! Contact Dr. Vivek Gupta & Dr. Ankit Bara immediately on our emergency helpline:

Severe Vomiting / Bloody Diarrhea Sign of Parvo or acute toxin ingestion.
Inability to Urinate Life-threatening urinary blockage (especially male cats).
Heavy Panting or Choking Heatstroke or foreign body airway blockage.
Road Trauma / Bleeding Urgent bleeding arrest and shock stabilization.
