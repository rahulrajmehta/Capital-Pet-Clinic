🐾 Puppy Immunization Program (6 Weeks to 1 Year)

Verified 2°C - 8°C cold-chain vaccines for Parvovirus, Distemper & Rabies with official digital health passport

1

6 - 8 Weeks: Puppy DP / Primodog (First Line of Defense)

Age: 45 - 60 Days

When puppies are weaned from mother's milk, their maternal antibody protection drops rapidly. This shot creates early defense against fatal Parvovirus enteritis and Canine Distemper.

Puppy DP (Parvo + Distemper) Deworming Syrup (1ml/kg) Health & Stool Screening
2

10 - 12 Weeks: 7-in-1 / 9-in-1 (DHPPiL) + Corona Booster

Age: ~75 Days

Expanded multi-disease protection covering Canine Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza, and 2 to 4 strains of Leptospirosis, plus Canine Coronavirus GI protection.

DHPPiL Dose 1 / Booster Canine Coronavirus Weight & Growth Evaluation
3

14 - 16 Weeks: Anti-Rabies (ARV) + Final DHPPiL Booster

Age: ~105 Days

Anti-Rabies is legally mandatory across India to prevent transmission of the fatal rabies virus. Accompanied by the final puppy DHPPiL booster and optional Kennel Cough (Bordetella).

Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) DHPPiL Booster 2 Kennel Cough Intranasal Safe to Walk Outdoors!
4

Annual Boosters & Routine Adult Protection

Every 12 Months Lifelong

Immunity wanes over 365 days. Adult dogs require 1 annual shot of Anti-Rabies and 1 annual shot of 9-in-1 combo, along with deworming tablets every 3 months.

Doctor's Advisory

Important Post-Vaccination Care Tips

Ensure a smooth and complication-free recovery for your pet after their vaccination appointment.

Mild Lethargy is Normal

It is completely natural for your pet to sleep more or show mild fever for 24-48 hours as their immune system responds to antibodies.

No Bathing for 48 Hours

Avoid giving your puppy or kitten a water bath for at least 48 to 72 hours post-vaccination to prevent hypothermia or immune stress.

Avoid Unknown Dogs

Do not take puppies to public parks or allow contact with stray or unvaccinated animals until all 3 primary puppy doses are completed.
